English

VeggieSonar (素食聲納) is a Hong Kong vegetarian/vegan dish finder.

Data we process

  • Place and dish listings you browse (from our catalog and, when available, Supabase).
  • Optional reports you submit (type, notes, optional contact).
  • Approximate location only if you grant permission (to sort by distance). Location is not uploaded to our servers by the app.
  • When you open Map, map tiles come from OpenStreetMap. OpenStreetMap receives requests for the approximate area you view and ordinary network information such as your IP address. If you allow location, the initial map area may be near you.
  • Purchase status for “Remove ads” via the store’s billing system (Google Play / App Store). We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Ad networks (e.g. Google AdMob) may collect device advertising identifiers when ads are shown. Remove-ads disables our banner placement.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not require an account to browse the catalog.

Sharing

  • Reports may be reviewed by the VeggieSonar operators to fix the catalog.
  • Store platforms and AdMob process data under their own policies when those features are used.
